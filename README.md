# Healthcare Analysis Dashboard

## Problem Statement

The goal of this project is to analyze healthcare data to identify high-risk patients based on healthcare data. Enabling better financial planning and patient care management.

---

## Dataset

The healthcare dataset includes:

* Patient details
* Medical conditions
* Admission & discharge dates
* Billing amount
* Insurance provider
* Risk classification (High / Low)

Dataset Link : https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/prasad22/healthcare-dataset

---

## Tools & Technologies

* Python (Pandas, NumPy)
* Power BI (Dashboard & Visualization)
* Data Analysis & Visualization

---

## Key Analysis

* High-risk vs low-risk patient cost comparison
* Cost distribution across medical conditions
* Insurance provider contribution analysis
* Monthly and Year-over-Year (YoY) cost trends

---

## Key Insights

* High-risk patients contribute significantly to total healthcare costs
* Healthcare costs show a rising trend over time
* Billing is concentrated among top insurance providers
* Certain conditions and treatments drive higher expenses

---

## Optimization Opportunities

* Reduce high-risk patient costs through preventive care
* Optimize length of hospital stay
* Control high-cost treatments and medications
* Negotiate better rates with key insurance providers

---

## Dashboard 

The dashboard includes:

* Key KPIs (Total Cost, Cost per Patient, Cost Ratio)
* Cost trend analysis (Monthly & YoY)
* Cost by condition, medication, and insurance provider
* Risk-based cost comparison
